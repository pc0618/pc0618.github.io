Hi there! I recently graduated from Stanford University with a fully funded master’s in Computer Science (AI concentration) and Distinction in Research. Advised by Percy Liang, I worked on the Marin project, an open-development lab training frontier open-source language models.

I’ve also worked on diffusion language models (block diffusion hybrids and efficient distributed context parallelism for block diffusion) and data for relational foundation models at Stanford, and on federated learning and AI in biomedicine at Argonne National Laboratory, including secure biomedical collaboration and privacy-preserving federated learning.

At Stanford, I’ve coauthored publications with professors Azalia Mirhoseini, Jure Leskovec, and Carlos Guestrin. At UIUC, I was fortunate to be advised by professors Eliu Huerta, Gagandeep Singh, Volodymyr Kindratenko, and Huan Zhang.

These days, I continue to work closely with Tarun Suresh and Hangoo Kang at Stanford on ML systems and architecture.
